# Amendment review surface

Mode: Operate, with source material available for reading.

Extend the workspace's established quiet white/green working surface into an offline
report. This surface introduces no marketing identity. Use system sans for controls
and prose, Georgia for the report title, tabular dates, dark green actions and visible
focus. No external fonts or media. Spacious headings separate dense legal evidence.

The first viewport names the amendment, shows whether approval is blocked, and starts
the actual change comparison. Change decisions sit beside their supporting wording.
Affected obligations follow with before/after dates, dependency reasons and calculation
traces. Source coverage and the final decision form complete the reading order.
On narrow screens the comparison becomes a vertical sequence. Native details controls
keep full JSON and provenance available without making the report unreadable.
